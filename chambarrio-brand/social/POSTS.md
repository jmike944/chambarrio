# Chambarrio · social media launch kit

Posts for Instagram and Facebook to start promoting the app. The images are in [`posts/`](posts/); the captions below are ready to copy and paste. Captions are in Spanish, written in the app's voice.

## Before the first post

- **Profile photo (both networks):** `whatsapp-profile-640.png` in this folder.
- **Facebook cover:** `posts/portada-facebook.png` (1640 x 924). Facebook trims the top and bottom on computers, so the text sits in the middle.
- **Instagram bio** (127 of 150 characters): El trabajo que buscas, cerca de ti. Vacantes a 2, 5, 10 o 15 km de tu casa en Saltillo y Ramos Arizpe. Gratis y sin currículum.
- **Link in the bio:** `https://chambarrio.com`
- **Facebook page button:** "Registrarte" or "Más información", pointing to `https://chambarrio.com`.

## Where the numbers come from

Every claim in the posts is taken from the app's own pages (`/` and `/empresas`):

- **$1,080 pesos and 26 hours a month** come from `monthlySavings(5)` in `src/lib/geo.ts`. If the commute model changes, re-render post 03 and story 2 and update the caption of post 03.
- **Prices for companies** are not printed on any image. The captions only say that publishing is free and that companies pay to see and contact applicants.
- The posts do not promise a number of vacancies.

## Changing a post

The images are drawn with the app's own art in `src/app/social-kit/page.tsx` (a development-only page).

```bash
npm run dev                      # then open http://localhost:3000/social-kit
node scripts/social-posts.mjs    # re-renders every PNG into posts/
```

The script needs Playwright with Chromium, which is not a project dependency.
